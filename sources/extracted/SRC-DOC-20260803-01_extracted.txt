[TABLE]

Sales area to sale office | TVKBZ | Sales office assignment to org unit. Used by order reports.

Vendor & customer data | LFA1 / LFB1 / KNA1 / KNB1 / KNVV / LFBK | Transporter (vendor) and customer master. Used in DI and Invoice (transporter, ship-to).
Mat. grp (Material Pricing Group) | T178 (KONDM) | Material pricing group. Feeds invoice pricing.
Nielsen Indicator | MARC / TNLS | Brand / Nielsen indicator (SC, Bangur, RC, Magna). Reporting & segmentation.
MatFrtGp (Material Freight Group) | TMFG / MFRGR | Material freight group (Cement Packed / Loose, Clinker, etc.). Used for shipment cost.
